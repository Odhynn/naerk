The closest and biggest moon of [[Naerk]], also called **the Cosmodrome** for its perceived mythical focal role in interplanar travel.

Even though it has been studied by the sages of Naerk since times immemorial, its exact contribution to the tidal forces and magical effects in various places around the globe is still sparsely explained as regards astronomical capacity, leaving the rest to speculation, and myth.

The temporal spans that characterise its orbit have been calculated as follows:
- Synodic Month (between new moons): 29.53 days
- Draconic Month (between orbital nodes): 27.21 days 
- Anomalistic Month (between perigees): 27.52 days 

Once every 20 years due to the synchronization between the lunar phase, nodal precession, and apsidal precession of Qusm (what some sages call the saros cycle), and the farther orbit of the smaller, irregular moon of Veth, Naerk enters a brief period of celestial instability.
This is marked by events like the [[Blood Moon]] and periodic conjunctions between Naerk, the larger, slower moon of [[Qusm]], the smaller, irregular moon [[Veth]], and the sun.
