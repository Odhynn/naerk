A remote city in a hidden valley, full of crops and agriculture. The land is fertile as it gets water from the [[Krantu]] river. After the pirate invasion in [[Whistor]] at 4817 it was excessively plundered and looted. The Duke's castle above the cliff was siezed and manned by a pirate garrison.
