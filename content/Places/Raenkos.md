A religious city of [[Karrokh]] with advanced trade and agriculture. It is believed to be the oldest city of [[Karrokh]].

## History

During the Treaty of Understanding, in the early 36th century, Raenkos grew as the most prominent and powerful city-state within [[Karrokh]]. For several centuries, the city was a major trade, spiritual and cultural center of the Inner Sea and influenced all nearby regions, from [[Lengoth]] to [[Zolkar]]. Philosophers, poets, writers and bards created marvelous works of art, still believed to be as some of the greatest.

The city declined at the rise of the [[Warclaw]] of the Fanged Keep. When they started spreading to the west, Raenkos' troops struggled to stop their advance. The gnolls broke the Treaty of Understanding and attacked the fertile plains at the border. Raenkos and the other city states started mobilizing troops and fortified themselves in the [[Twins]]. However, at the [[Battle of Twin Mountains]], they were overwhelmed by massive enemy gnoll forces and few managed to escape the massacre. Raenkos was subsequently besieged and famine spread like wildfire within the city walls. The city walls held, but it is estimated nine out of ten citizens died over the next decades, mostly due to malnourishment and skirmishes to protect the city.

After the [[Warclaw]] rule, at the 43th century, [[Kindasir]] grew to be the new capital of [[Karrokh]].
