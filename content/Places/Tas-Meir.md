An independent gnoll city-state of [[Karrokh]]. During the early 40th century, the [[Warclaw]] established their base in the Fanged Keep and started spreading to the west and north. Their rule lasted almost two centuries, and claimed most territories in [[Karrokh]] and [[Green Hills]].
