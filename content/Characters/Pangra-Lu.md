Founding dragon of [[Dragonpact]]
Royal bloodline
Esteemed Visitor

An ancient and slender wyrm, carries immense wisdom and power for millenia. It is believed he attacked the kingdom of [[Athalaar]]. His efforts though were in vein, as even he could not alter the power of prophecy of what was to come. Tales say of his enormous size, as long as a river, of his foresight for the troubles to come in [[Athalahr]] and of his cunning, able to elude the Harrowmist.
