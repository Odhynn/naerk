A warpriest of [[Karadosh]] that fought fierce battles in [[North Darrok]] against the [[Guldorom Horde]]. Using his battle magic, he fought with heavy armor, longsword and tower shield along with his allies. During the [[War of the Titan]] he took active part in the defence of his village, [[Belaroth]], killing several trolls and bodyguards of [[Zlo'ttan]]. Among his companions were [[Hawk]], [[Amanthir]] and [[Dalzak]].
