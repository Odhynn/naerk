Harhundun is the marshal of the [[Void Plane]]'s incursion in the haunted continent of [[Athalaar]], leading the hordes of the aberrations there there as a representative of [[Yast]].
His form has seldom remained the same, but always embodies a tortured appearance twisted by eldritch energies; to gaze upon it is to court madness.
