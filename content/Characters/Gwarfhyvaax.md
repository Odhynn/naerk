Gwarfhyvaax (gwarf-hee-VAAKS) — or Gwarfhyvijx (gvar-fee-VAIKS) as the Alagonese sailors would call him in their West Oerdang tongue — is the ancient horned dragon who inherited the stewardship over the Mitheryn mistwoods in Athalaar shortly after the demise of his predecessor, the sovereign dragon Fe Leng Da Xu (or Ferrendijx).
In his forest lair, he conserved a trove of ancient literature from the fallen Kingdom of Selanaar.

When the [[7th Silver Flame Sentinels]] arrived in Edhailin in late 4784, they stumbled through the woods until a kirin led them to Gwarfhyvaax.
He bade them drive the young forest dragon [[Lhan Xi]] off his territory, in exchange for the guidance necessary for their acclimatisation to Naerk. Once they had done so, they remained under his tutelage for 11 months and even snuck a peek into his hidden library.

The serenity he had known for most of his life within his own primal pocket in the haunted continent was shattered when the Sennarii arrived.
Allowing the majority of their elven population (who later formed the Clans of Mist) to inhabit his territory only came at the end of direct threats from the [[Angaeradh|Prophetess Under the Mountain]].
Eventually he grudgingly settled into coexistence with the humanoids, coming to enjoy their reverence, offerings, and learned discourse, as well as his honourary seat at the helm of the druidic [[Circle of the Stag]].
