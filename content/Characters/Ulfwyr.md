Ulfwyr Arthwyrsson is a field agent of the [[Berenfaer]] by the handle "Teucer", a scout sniper longbowman well-versed in guerilla warfare, a cynical paramedic, and one of the [[Everbryght]].

- speaks common with a slight North Darroki accent
- caustic eloquence
- holds silent grudges
- has taught his dire wolf companion, Virginia, to growl and sit on interrogees
- smells of rain

## Berenfaer

He was a major actor in the [[Zolkar's Dawn Riot]] events that led to the resurgence of the Berenfaer, under the guidance of [[Steelshade]] and alongside [[Black Mist]].

## South Sea

Later that year, he sailed the South Sea alongside his brother [[Bjonnyr]], eventually reaching as far as [[Drozzin]], where they fought and killed the sea dragon [[Ozrallon]].
