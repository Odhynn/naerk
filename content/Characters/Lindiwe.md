Lindiwe ap Lwgh was once a [[Lleyn#Maidens of the Lake|Maiden of the Lake]] and a potential successor to the 65th Lady of the Lake, before the latter perished upon the Arrival of the Sennarii and ascended to divinity as [[Lleyn]].
As was customary for Maidens of the Lake, in order to prepare for a lifetime of site-bound service, she travelled across Naerk and visited many places, while also returning to Edhailin from time to time to pay her respects to the Lady and help guide any extraterrestrial visitors (such as [[7th Silver Flame Sentinels]]) off Athalaar.
When the portal of Edhailin was broken, a long line of Ladies of the Lake came to an end and she was free to live out her life however she deemed.

Pursuant to her globetrotting nature, she soon became one of the foremost naval explorers of the [[Syvhorms Cartographic Society]] and instrumental in their coastline mapping efforts of the 4790s-4800s.
In the summer of 4817, while on such a voyage, she was shipwrecked on [[Drozzin]], a continent that had thus far eluded notice, much less cartographic purview, of the old world.
