A comedically shrewd propagandist, perceptive analyst of group dynamics, self-proclaimed vigilante and political officer of the Kobold Kommonwealth[^1], the spymaster known as **Komrade Kobold** or by his [[Berenfaer]] handle **"Kommissar"** has forgotten his real name after a particularly nasty fall down three or four flights of stairs - though he does recall it began with "K". <!-- it was Klouseau x) I regret nothing -->

Born to a redscale kobold tribe in the Ennis mountains of Southern Darrok, shortly after the incident he departed, denouncing the oppressive rule of the tribe's red dragon patron. Or rather, he denounced, departing.

He spent a few years in the underbelly of Ekteron, where he became even more radicalised through his involvement with Kobold Kommonwealth groups, and eventually stowed away on a ship bound for Zolkar. There he became involved with the Berenfaer and set upon rallying the local people and kobolds to cast off their shackles, orating in his distinct southern darroki accent.

[^1]: The Kobold Kommonwealth is a fledgling iconoclastic movement across kobold communities that challenges existing power structures (in the face of a general kobold tendency for adherence to them) and seeks to unionise against the draconic status-quo.
