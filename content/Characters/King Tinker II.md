The half-elf king from [[Runolf]] is believed to be a refugee from [[Tithlon]] that grew popular among the southern provinces of [[Green Hills]] for his resolve against the invading skinwalkers and gnolls of [[Rammok Wastes]], as well as his ability to criticise [[Queen Talla IV]]'s inability to rule while being able to stay out of her grasp.
