Szaulthov is the marshal of the [[Shadow Plane]]'s incursion in the haunted continent of [[Athalaar]], leading the hordes of the undead there as a representative of [[Voolthow]].
He appears as a gaunt graveknight whose eyes burn with a malicious ghostly light, and whose armour constantly exudes a pale fog eerily similar to the Harrowmist.
