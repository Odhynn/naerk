A faithful warrior and devoted servant of [[Yerastu]] clergy in the Temple of the Vigilant Guard, [[Ilistair]]. He fought as a young knight against the [[Bloodscale]] attack at year 4777 and killed several enemy warriors. 

In the fateful year 4788, a [[Kenemi]], [[Sorevial]], was restationed to guard the Temple of the Vigilant Guard. The two ignored strict prohibitions as they joined their mortal and immortal blood. When the respective Orders found out that she was pregnant, they conducted a trial and decided to punish them both. After a divine dictum, they both lost their voice for 25 years (a generation) and were tasked with the most demanding duties. They were torn apart once again, as Sorevial was sent back to [[Branstraz]], in [[Olgizmar]], while Sir Polakram was sent to [[Four Winds Monastery]] as a common mute guard.
