A sworn knight and vice general of the [[Imtaria|Imtarian]] army. Young but already accomplished and expert in several weapons and tactics. He was seen observing arenas and he is prone to gambling, although he opposes slavery. His office headquarters are in [[Syvorms]]. Husband of [[Lindar]] Estalarri, the Leader of the Pactkeepers. It is believed he indirectly supported the riot leaders of [[Zolkar]] during the [[Zolkarsdawn Riot]].
