A child born from the unfortunate union of two servants of [[Yerastu]]. He was given to a temple of the faith in [[Bránstraž]] and there he learned the tenets of the god and tended to several activities of the divine priests. He left the clergy at the age of 15 and travelled to [[Dorvolosh]], where he got involved into several illegal activities.
