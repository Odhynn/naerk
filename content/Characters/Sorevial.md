In the fateful year 4788, [[Sorevial]], was tasked to guard the Temple of the Vigilant Guard in [[Ilistair]]. She met Sir [[Polakram]] and they ignored strict prohibitions as they joined their mortal and immortal blood. When the respective Orders found out that she was pregnant, they conducted a trial and decided to punish them both. After a divine dictum, they both lost their voice for 25 years (a generation) and were tasked with the most demanding duties. They were torn apart once again, as Sorevial was sent back to [[Bránstraž]], in [[Olgizmar]], while Sir Polakram was sent to [[Four Winds Monastery]] as a common mute guard.
