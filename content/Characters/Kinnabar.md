Kinnabar the Red, a.k.a. Komrade Kobold, is a spymaster of the [[Berenfaer]] by the handle "Kommissar" and self-proclaimed vigilante and political officer of the Kobold Kommonwealth, a sporadic movement across kobold communities that seeks to unionise against mistreatment by the draconic status-quo.
His comedic stature notwithstanding, he is a shrewd propagandist and observant analyst of group dynamics and spatial surroundings.
