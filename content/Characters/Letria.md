The aunt of [[Emperor Nalangis II]] is considered to be a fine politician and diplomat. She is believed to have played a crucial role for the establishment of the young emperor. She is in charge of a complex spy network and is the backbone of the [[Imtaria|Imtarian]] foreign policy. She harbors a special hate for the Bildarion family of [[Hashnev]], more than ever after the events of [[Hashnev, Revolt and Betrayal]].
