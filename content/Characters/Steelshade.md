Coucillor **Pieter-Jan Steelshade** is one of the two envoys of Zolkar in the Council of [[Palaurim]], senior spymaster of the [[Berenfaer]] and their political representative.
He is credited with spearheading the reformation of the Berenfaer in the events of [[Zolkarsdawn Riot]], before which he served as the guildmaster of the Elestari Smith's Guild.

His methods were sometimes quite unusual and questionable but the results are indisputable.


Under his leadership in the years leading up to the [[Zolkarsdawn Riot]], he reformed the previously fringe revolutionary group into a competent militant organisation; in the months following the riot, they worked to dismantle the slave trade in Zolkar and set their sights even further.
Representing Zolkar for the first time in the Council of Palaurim, convened in [[Hashnev]] over the spring of 4817, he brought attention to the persistent problem of human trafficking in Palaurim and especially Arbader.
In doing so he stood out too much in insidious Hashnev; his precautions eventually failed, and he ended up in thrall to the vampire coven of the Church of Xaurim for many weeks, before eventually freed as part of a prisoner exchange negotiated between the vampires and [[Aileen]] Rosewood[^1], a young arcanist who had helped Steelshade expose the slave trade to the council.
Weakened and exhausted, he inducted Aileen into the Berenfaer as before returning to Hashnev once the council drew to a close.

He soon retired from active duty and relinquished leadership of the Berenfaer to the assembly of spymasters, the most talented and accomplished of agents. While he still sits at the head of the assembly, he is mostly preoccupied with his diplomatic duties as a councillor of Palaurim and the political face of the Berenfaer.[^2]

[^1]: Terrified out her wits by the ease of Steelshade's capture, Aileen aggressively pushed for major reforms in espionage methods, eventually establishing the safety protocols by which the zolkari Berenfaer operate today.
[^2]: Every agent adopted a callsign to use instead of their name except Steelshade; it made little sense to use a secret handle when he was synonymous with the Berenfaer in the public sphere. Soon this came to be true of Aileen too, when she became the cultural face of the Berenfaer and harpist muse of Elestair.
