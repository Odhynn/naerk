Coucillor **Pieter-Jan Steelshade** is one of the two representatives of Zolkar in the Council of [[Palaurim]] and a head spymaster of the [[Berenfaer]].
He is credited with spearheading the reformation of the Berenfaer in the events of [[Zolkar's Dawn Riot]], before which he served as the guildmaster of the Elestari Smith's Guild.

His methods were sometimes quite unusual and questionable but the results are indisputable.
Having been in the public eye since the very beginning, he is the only Berenfaer not to use an agent handle or *nom-de-guerre*, as any Zolkari citizen or continental diplomat worth their salt know who he is.

Under his leadership in the years leading up to the [[Zolkar's Dawn Riot]], he reformed the previously fringe revolutionary group into a competent militant organisation; in the months following the riot, they dismantled the slave trade in Zolkar and set their sights further.
Representing Zolkar for the first time in the Council of Palaurim, convened in [[Hashnev]] over the spring of 4817, he brought attention to the persistent problem of human trafficking in Arbader.
In doing so he stood out too much in corrupt and shady Hashnev; his precautions eventually failed, and he ended up enthralled by the vampire coven of the Church of Xaurim for weeks before being rescued by [[Aileen]] Rosewood, a young arcanist who had helped him expose the slave trade to the council.
Weakened and exhausted, he inducted Aileen into the Berenfaer before returning to Hashnev once the council drew to a close.

He soon retired from active duty and relinquished leadership of the Berenfaer to the assembly of spymasters, the most talented and accomplished of agents. While he still sits at the head of the assembly, he is mostly preoccupied with his diplomatic duties as a councillor of Palaurim and the political face of the Berenfaer.
