Councillor **Pieter-Jan Steelschijd**, known as Steelshade, is one of the two envoys of Zolkar in the Council of [[Palaurim]], senior spymaster of the [[Berenfaer]] and their political representative.
He is credited with spearheading the reformation of the Berenfaer in the events of [[Zolkarsdawn Riot]], before which he served as the guildmaster of the Elestari Smith's Guild.

Though his methods were sometimes quite unusual and questionable, the results were indisputable.


Under his leadership in the years leading up to the [[Zolkarsdawn Riot]], he reformed the previously fringe revolutionary group into a competent militant organisation; in the months following the riot, they worked to dismantle the slave trade in Zolkar and set their sights even further.
Representing Zolkar in the last Council of Palaurim, convened in [[Hashnev]] over the spring of 4817, he brought attention to the persistent problem of human trafficking within the alliance and especially Arbader.
In doing so he stood out too much in insidious Hashnev. His precautions eventually failed, and he ended up in thrall to the vampire coven of the Church of Xaurim for many weeks. Eventually he was released as part of a prisoner exchange negotiated between the vampires and [[Aileen]] Rosewood[^1], a young Berenfaer initiate who had helped Steelshade expose the slave trade to the council.
Weakened and exhausted, once the council drew to a close he let Aileen handle the Arbaderi situation Berenfaer and returned to Elestair to recuperate.

He soon retired from active duty and relinquished leadership of the Berenfaer to the assembly of spymasters, the most talented and accomplished of agents. While he still sits at the head of the assembly, he is mostly preoccupied with his diplomatic duties as a councillor of Palaurim and the political face of the Berenfaer.

[^1]: Terrified out of her wits by how easily Steelshade was captured and enthralled (not to mention all the derivative security issues), Aileen aggressively pushed for major reforms in espionage methods, eventually establishing the safety protocols by which the Zolkari branch of the Berenfaer operates today.
