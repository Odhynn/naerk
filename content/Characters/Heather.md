Heather is two-centuries old manic changeling witch that travels Naerk in search of horticultural materials, rarely staying in one place for more than one season.
She is a member of the [[Circle of the Stag]] in Mitheryn forest, and known to other druidic circles as a skilled shapeshifter; given her attained power and the weight of her years, she might even have been the circle's leader if only she did not come off as a hyperactive adolescent perpetually high on tea.
She bruises with supernatural ease.

## Background

Hárþinur was one of the [[Bjåglant|Bjåglanders']] most remote settlements up the Draugrelfr river, suddenly plagued by strange supernatural occurences near the close of the 46th century. The occultist Skinge Gyddardottir of the Hyrr clan was dispatched to investigate. Her search ultimately led her to the discovery of a maddening curse that had been awakened there. Skinge and the villagers fled Hárþinur, yet the curse followed, manifesting again years later. Fear of it spreading surged within their new communities, and they accursed were ousted across the Draugrelfr into the land of ghosts.
Skinge was pregnant at the time, and ended up trekking alone through the misty woods, bitter and tormented. It was then that she came upon an ancient menhir covered in moss, when the pains took her. Teetering in the brink of madness, she laid before the standing stone an offering of purple heather flowers, gave birth to a sickly girl, and dedicated it to whatever elven spirits watched over that ancient shrine, with her dying breath.

Her plea was answered by [[Cernunnos]] himself. The stag-horned golden emissary crossed the veil into the material world, took the newborn babe in his arms, and saw that it was a sorrow may, a changeling born of a magically gifted woman driven to hatred and haunted by a curse most foul.
It was then that he heard eerie footsteps approach, and turned to regard the [[Angaeradh|Doomsayer]] herself. She had come for the child as much as he had. He refused to deliver it. Angaeradh then revealed that she had foreseen a great destiny for the changeling: she would be the one to research and concoct the Cure. However, that would be a task spanning centuries; a length of time the girl did not have, but that Angaeradh as a warden of Time could give her. It was imperative for the salvation of [[Athalaar]], she declared, and so it fell entirely within her jurisdiction. She beckoned once more for the babe.
Cernunnos refused yet again. They were both striving for the salvation of Athalaar, yet he had not salvaged the child from one kind of corruption only to condemn it to another. He reminded Angaeradh that those were his menhirs, and therefore the child was dedicated to him. If its longevity was imperative, he declared, then it fell to him to find another way to that.

Thus he arranged for the newborn to be taken as far away from Athalaar as possible, to ensure that the evil spirits that permeated the tormented continent forget about her. Heather was raised in an enchanted fey glade in the highland woods near [[St. Qurin]], watched over by a coven of hags, pixies and other creatures of the primal world. The inhabitants of the glade were tasked with preparing her for the journey to the Fountain of Youth, a mythical locale deep in the Jungles of the Sun God that was said to confer eternal youth, but only if the petitioner was worthy and possessing knowledge of an ancient and obscure rite.
In an attempt to sabotage this endeavour and force Cernunnos to acquiesce to her alternative, Angaeradh leaked intelligence about the Fountain of Youth through her agents to certain officers in the Imtarian Army Expedition to Zelgerosh, at the time embroiled in fighting against an insurgency in the wider colony of St. Qurin. One of them, Knight-Captain Merren Carrevert succeeded in capturing 10-year-old Heather amidst the fighting, and interrogated her seeking further leads to the Fountain. He was stopped by petty officer Sgt. [[Felix]] Mason, who knocked out most of his teeth and carried Heather back to her glade, giving his life in the process.
When Heather came of age, she undertook the perilous journey to the Fountain of Youth. After many trials and ordeals, she became one of the precious few ever to drink of the stygian waters, yet discovered that things were not so simple; eternal youth came with a neurotic energy that threatened to break her mind and awaken the curse in her blood. She has been thenceforth suppressing her own feral transformation by constant consumption of special herbal tea.

Returning to Athalaar, she studied under the [[Lleyn|65th Lady of the Lake]], the samsaran witch guardian of Edhailin, before setting out into the world accompanied by one of the Lady's Maidens, [[Lindiwe]] ap Lwgh, on the arduous horticultural task of finding the right materials and discovering the correct procedures for the brewing of the Cure. 200 years and thousands of failed experiments later, her quest is not even halfway done, but she travels still.
