Lhan Xi is a young forest dragon from the western Athar woods. His ambitions to an appropriately lush territory in the pristine wilds of what is now Sennarin were met by the unyielding claim of [[Gwarfhyvaax]], the ancient horned dragon of Mitheryn. 
Unable to get the old wyrm to give him part of his vast wooded domain, the young dragon resorted to defiance. He hunted in Gwarfhyvaax's forest for prey, and upturned the ancient ruins for treasure. When the furious old wyrm would come in hot, Lhan Xi would simply cackle and disappear among the tree boughs with his youthful mobility and subterfuge.

Unable to pin the young upstart down without a great deal of effort, Gwarfhyvaax was beginning to worry that this challenge to his claim might eventually solidify, when the [[7th Silver Flame Sentinels]] arrived into his grounds, fresh off the astral paths from the Realms Beyond and in need of guidance.
In return for taking them under his wing, Gwarfhyvaax bade them rid him of the nuisance of Lhan Xi.

Upon his first encounter with the band, Lhan Xi was delighted and offered to become their patron instead if they would help rid him of the old wyrm. When they declined he figured it was ages since he had humanoid flesh anyway. His delighted onslaught soon turned to frustrated escape, as he realised the humanoids were too strong to take head on.
Thus, he employed his tried and true guerilla tactics, heckling their efforts to pin him down throughout the misty woods and seaside cliffs.

Eventually, the band tracked him down to his lair in a dewy island just off the coast. One final attempt to nip at them through the thick fog failed, and he could only escape in defeat, gravely injured, while they looted his hoard.
