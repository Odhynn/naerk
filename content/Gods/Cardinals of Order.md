Among the most revered servants of [[Yerastu]] are the Cardinals of Order. These high-ranking [[Kenemi]] are ever vigilant and steadfast in routing out instances of chaos within the [[Material Plane]]. Each one is specialized in dealing with specific problems that may arise.
