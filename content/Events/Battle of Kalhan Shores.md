The earliest recorded attempt of [[Flame Gulf]] and [[Markesh]] to invade and attack [[Whistor]]. They sent numerous ships, disguised as merchant trader caravels. They reached the shores of [[Kalhan]] without suspicion and they started unloading troops on the island to take it by surprise. The warriors headed south to attack [[Marjalat]], however several brigades were lost in the [[Alta Woods]]. After several hours they reached the city, however some hunters had informed the city of the incoming army. The gates were closed and the walls manned with the local militia. The first warriors were greeted with arrow volleys and stopped in their tracks. Without cover, a lot of them were killed in the spot, until they retreated in the woods to reorganise.

A fleet arrived next morning from [[Sjemhe]], with thirty ships. Together with ten ships from Marjalat, they attacked the anchored [[Markesh]] fleet, left only with a sceleton crew. Most of them were easy pickings, and almost thirty ships were captured before even raising their anchor. The fight was brief, as the invader ships tried to flee towards the open ocean. More were sunk or captured, with multiple casualties. The warriors that were hiden on the forest were left with no ships and almost no supplies. Skirmish attacks further depleted their reserves. They tried to break the encirclement, but to no avail. Four days afterwards, they got out of the woods and surrendered.
