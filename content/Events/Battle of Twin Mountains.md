When the Great Mother of the [[Warclaw]] established herself in the Fanged Keep of [[Tas-Meir]], they started spreading like wildfire to the west. The Treaty of Understanding with [[Karrokh]] was violated after several organised attacks on merchant caravans. The gnolls spread towards the [[Twins]] and the armies of [[Raenkos]], though well equipped, were struggling to contain them before the reached the fertile western plains. A few skirmishes on the desert reduced their number, so they built fortifications on the mountains and waited there.

The two armies met in the hills that connect the [[Twins]]. A never ending flow of rampaging gnolls attacked the defending army of [[Raenkos]] and the soldiers, although brave and disciplined, were unable to stop the tide of fur and claws. More flanked them from the north and south, and eventually the majority of the troops were encircled and cut down. Only 6000 managed to escape, many among them in a bad shape.

This defeat marks the end of the Treaty of Understanding and the decline of the city state of [[Raenkos]].
