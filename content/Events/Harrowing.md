Athalaar was wracked by the disaster of *the Harrowing* in 3260, when the veil between the Material and Ethereal planes was torn and the horrors that lurked beyond, both eldritch and deathless, surged through and throughout the continent, snuffing out the great Kingdom of Selanaar and its civilisation in a matter of a few days of unspoken terror, carnage, and death. 

The afflicted lands were first beset by a deathly glowing fog, the [[Harrowmist]], which turned every living creature it engulfed into an writhing lifeless husk. On the wake of the Harrowmist came the aberrations and the undead, collectively named **the Horror Hordes** turning the thralls to their own ranks and setting them to war between them.

### Containment
This apocalyptic outpour was contained within Athalaar by the desperate actions of the new Prophetess Under the Mountain, [[Angaeradh]].
Drawing upon ancient elven magic, Angaeradh tapped into the underground ley line network, the nodes of which coalesced in the _[[Athalaar#The Maenhir|maenhir]]_ standing stones. 

Before the Harrowing could spill into the rest of Naerk, she thus raised an impassable barrier around Athalaar, cutting it off from the world.

This happened so fast that none that resided within Athalaar was able to get out in time, and millions of refugees were shut in before they could escape.
Some dashed their ships against the barrier, some stood their ground in vain against the Harrowmist and the Horror Hordes; save from those in the southernmost and easternmost regions, all perished.

In her first major act as the ruler of what remained of Athalaar, Angaeradh had quarantined the entire continent and doomed her subjects to the ravages of the Harrowing, in order to spare the rest of the world from it.
