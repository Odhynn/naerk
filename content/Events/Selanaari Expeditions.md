The Clans of Snow, as the [[Sennarin|Senarii]] clan confederation closest to the haunted lands of Selanaar, organised two ambitious ventures to explore and research them during the temporal window of opportunity created by the Blood Moon, when the Harrowmist retreats for one lunar cycle before surging back. Two expeditions were undertaken in the two Blood Moon cycles since the Arrival: the first during Leafdrop of 4796, the second during Leafdrop of 4816. Both ended in disaster and unspeakable carnage.


The First Expedition in 4796 was effectively an arms race against the [[Bjåglant|Bjåglander]] clans, as both sides sought to gain an edge in the Arrival Wars by uncovering the secrets of the once mighty Selanaari Empire first.
After a nebulous turn of events the Sennarii force was eventually cut off from supplies and perished to the last man in Fort Fuyuhan, subsequently turning into shades.
The Bjåglander force was similarly decimated by the horrors of the land, and only Phaenniel, the cassissian angel familiar of a wizard researcher, survived trapped in the Carven Keep.


After the end of the Arrival Wars, and in the spirit of fostering cooperation and coexistent among the former belligerents, the Second Expedition was organised to cautiously investigate what had transpired. The joint naval venture was deployed by the Sennarii Clans of Mist, the Sennarii Clans of Snow, and the Bjåglander Clans of Spökeborg, Aarhall, and Bärshamn.
The commanders and sages of the expedition were hoping to land on eastern shores of the Selanaari peninsula, decrypt the workings of the ancient menhir wards well enough to activate a natural barrier against the Harrowmist similar to what was protecting the coastal goblinoid communities, and subsequently consolidate a scientific foothold on the haunted ancient lands.
Disaster, however, struck well before the expedition even landed, as the seas turned savage, dashing most of the joint fleet on the craggy coastal shores, where the survivors fell prey to oni attacks.

Over those same days on a different coast to the south, a group of unaffiliated castaways that would eventually be the [[Halcyon]] Band washed ashore and haphazardly made their way through hostile kobold territory towards the goblin coasts, where they came across the wrecked Mist clan ships. Trudging forward through the haunted bogs, they reached the Carven Keep, where they found Phaenniel and freed it.
As the Blood Moon progressed, however, the Harrowmist started to reemerge and close in, bringing in its wake a horde of spectres and abberations, the warring hosts of the Haruspices of Voolthow and Yast. In an advanced stage of infection by the Harrowmist taint, the Bjåglander dwarf Rhägar stayed behind to buy the band time to flee.

Traversing through the haunted remains of the Selanaari mountain villages, the Halcyon band finally reached the ancient castle of [[Lohrtirion]] high in the mountains, where the Sennarii Expedition was dug in, decimated by oni attacks, undead haunts, and eldritch horrors over the past few days.
Among them stood the [[Everbryght]] youngsters, who joined the fierce last stand of the Sennarii against the onslaught of the advancing spectre horde, except for [[Aileen]] Rosewood, who instead was tasked to climb the ancient communications tower of the castle. Exhausting her dimensional stepping magic, she managed to reach the top and transmit their location to the rescue fleet, but was subsequently stranded there and was infected by the Harrowmist.

Meanwhile, the Halcyon Band had entrenched themselves in the ruins of a cliff-side shrine. There, as their bandmates [[Orok]], [[Olsarthis]] Feltharyn, and the Sennarii blademaidens Fuurindel and Yuugen held off the underworld dragon Fang Zhin and his shadow minions, [[Ternath]] Inkblood and [[Ulsath]] Feltharyn successfully completed a ritual that released [[Angaeradh]] from her centuries-old arcane confinement to [[Eltirion]].
Unbound, the Prophetess swooped down to confront the Haruspex of Voolthow, and the Halcyon Band made a desperate run for the last defensive line of the expedition. Fuurindel succumbed to a petrifying affliction she had contracted in the Carven Keep to free Faenniel, while the rest of the party had no choice but to sprint through the Harrowmist during their escape, and contracted the Harrowing Curse themselves.

Their actions bought enough time for the remnants of the expedition to hold out until the [[Sennarii Griffon Cavalry|Griffon Cavalry]] of the Snow Clans could arrive and extract them to the rescue fleet.
Though the Second Expedition failed to gain a foothold, and Selanaar remained no less a haunted interplanar battlefield than it was before, precious intel was recovered through Phaenniel's reports and Angaeradh's imparted knowledge.
Clan officials have thus far refused to provide specifics about that knowledge or say whether they plan to hold a third expedition upon the next Blood Moon in 4836.
