The established calendar system in civilised Naerk observes a solar year of 364 days, beginning from the winter solstice and split into 12 months, each consisting of 30 days.
Initially, solstices and equinoctes were not included in the months, but for the sake of simplicity they were eventually subsumed in the first month of each season, making them 31 days long.

1. Nightfall (31 days) (Voolthow'n)
2. Coldreign (30 days) (an'Fallfaunt)
3. Snowmelt (30 days) (Volrek'an)
4. Flowergrow (31 days) (Malahir'an)
5. Seedsowing (30 days) (Factar'an)
6. Sunrise (30 days) (Reszek'an)
7. Fireheart (31 days) (an'Laerion)
8. Moondance (30 days) (Sumril'an)
9. Grogfeast (30 days) (Nahema'n)
10. Rainfalls (31 days) (Yerastu'n)
11. Leafdrop (30 days) (a'Disiklek)
12. Graymist (30 days) (Amehann)

The solar cycle of Naerk is slightly longer than 364 days, leading to a shift in accuracy regarding the solstices and equinoctes.
To offset this, sages established the introduction of 25 leap days in Leafdrop upon every 20-year cycle, to tie in with the same uncannily periodic alignment of [[Qusm]] and [[Veth]].
This is called the [[Blood Moon]], and lasts from 31 to 55 Leafdrop every 20th year (last one being 4816).

## Other conventions

In some regions mostly inhabited by barbarians and nomads, time is reckoned by more empirical metrics instead, as in with seasons in the North (Fallas = winter, Volont = spring, Perlon = summer, Livdash = autumn) or lunar cycles in the Athar Grounds.

####  Festivals

Different regions observe different holidays based on significant political, historic or religious events.
In Palaurim, common observances are:
- 10 Flowergrow: Anniversary of the founding of Palaurim
- 23 Moondance: Anniversary of the signing of the Dragonpact.
- 16 Leafdrop: Peace Day, in Oromil.
- 2 Coldreign: Day of the Dead, in the North and Thimvert.
- 1 Flowergrow (spring solstice): Tanrez, Day of the Twin Walkers, in the Green Hills.
