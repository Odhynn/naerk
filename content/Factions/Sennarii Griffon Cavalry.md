The signature military unit of the Snow Clans of [[Sennarin]] is their Griffon Cavalry, called **Shijuu Kiheidan** (獅鷲騎兵隊 "lion-eagle mounted corps") in Sennarii, and are also colloquially known as Sennarii Snowriders to outsiders.
It is an airborne task force consisting of four squadrons of armoured griffon riders, typically wielding the bladed polearms known as _naginata_ for melee engagements and strafes, as well as asymmetrical _daikyu_ bows for ranged suppression. The griffon mounts typically wear light barding and double as fearsome combatants themselves.

The unit was developed through many years of effort by the Snow Clans to strike an accord with the griffon population of the Shiijyu mountains and safeguard their nesting grounds from the local Oni tribes, who prize griffon eggs as a delicacy.
They only saw active duty during the last stretch of the [[Sennarin#the Arrival Wars|Arrival Wars]] in 4804[^1].


[^1]: A popular tall tale in Yoakyuuden holds that the dwarven mountain clans in [[Bjåglant]] had been aware of the existence of the Nordarroki Griffonriders in the Tarhelm mountains for three decades by then, and were looking to develop such a unit themselves; when they found out that the Sennarii had beaten them to it, the dwarves got so mad they lit their beards on fire, and immediately filed for peace afterwards so that they would not have to go into battle without facial hair.
