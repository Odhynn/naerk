The 7th generation of the Silver Flame Sentinels were:
- Ruthar Laffirdul, a talented wizard (incantator)
- Szoraufein, dark elf guerilla archer 
- [[Maladrok]] Kelegrim, human fiendslayer crusader
- Aaron Kunbart, human paladin of Reszek

They journeyed across Naerk during their quest for the Dawnblade, a powerful artifact sword belonging to a fallen archangel from the Realms Beyond, that was sought after by Faenyss Laffirdul, [[Jeanne Claire Lillith]], and the devil Griannimhziel.

They were aided in their quest by:
- Lyndria, high priestess of Nahema
- Sigurd, northman warchief of Fervolosh

## Background

In the Realms Beyond there used to stand Garth Brethil, a remote citadel populated by silver elves tasked with guarding dangerous arcane secrets by that world's goddess of magic.
The Incantatrix, the archmage wielding those powers was traditionally female, and protected by a retinue of 3 close companions; this group was known as the Sentinels of the Silver Flame.

After the a powerful wizard cabal attacked Garth Brethil, the secrets were stolen and the 5th generation of Silver Flame Sentinels killed off. Mortally wounded, the last member of the 5th picked 4 of the surviving youths and anointed them as the 6th generation in a ritual that completed with her dying breath.
Those were:
- Aerlinniel Allaer (incantatrix)
- Faenyss Laffirdul
- Annwn Raerundlin
- Inthiir Allaer

Heavy was their duty: the goddess of magic commanded that they reclaim the dangerous secrets; until they did, none of them could return to Garth Brethil.
After a century of struggle, sacrifices, hopeless wandering, and hard choices, Inthiir was slain, Aerlinniel went mad after revisiting Garth Brethil to seek answers, and Annwn had left the group. By the end, only one wizard of the cabal remained.

Aerlinniel was killed by Faenyss after she nearly their son Ruthar in her madness.
Faenyss forced the last cabal wizard to pass on the forbidden knowledge to Ruthar; when this was done after Ruthar came of age many decades later, he had the wizard killed.

Ruthar was by curious strands of fate joined in his early fights against tyranny by companions whose fathers or mentors were, like Faenyss, previously involved with the Dawnblade.

When Annwn realised that Faenyss was after the archangel swords, she anointed Ruthar along with his companions as the 7th generation of Silver Flame Sentinels in the ruins of Garth Brethil.

They spent the next 5 years there constructing an astral portal to transport them to Naerk, where the [[Dawnblade]] lay.

## Quest for the Dawnblade

They arrived in the Lake of the Moon in late 4784 and wandered through the oni territories and Mitheryn forest. A kirin ultimately led them before Gwarfhyvijx, the green dragon of the forest.
In an accord with Gwarfhyvijx, they helped drive his opponent, the young forest dragon Lhan Xi off his lands, in exchange for guidance and tutelage that lasted for a year.

Travelling through the [[Athar]] orc tribe territories, they eventually boarded a ship from [[Bjåglant]] to the [[Alagon]] archipelago, where they visited the Temple of the Sapphire Guard and found traces of a halfling named Tanner, whom Faenyss Laffirdul had killed 50 years ago on his first trip to Naerk while chasing Çan Tal, the person who had carried the Dawnblade there.

The band then followed the leads to Tanner's tower in Ekteron, acquiring the property from his devil accountants and establishing the Elysium Guild around it. On the way they undertook the ascent to the Four Winds Monastery to consult the Oracle.

In spring of 4786, after months of preparation and research, the companions journeyed to [[Olgizmar]], where they briefly worked for the Cwmri circle to gather insider information. Before the magisters could properly sniff out their true purposes, they set out to the demon-held territories, where they befriended the Troll tribe confederation and slew the lesser demon lord Knaridzna while he was out on patrol looking for them.
Knaridzna's demise thoroughly shook the demons into shoring up their complacent defences, while Maladrok Kelegrim and Lyndria single-handedly slew two linnorms tied to [[Jeanne Claire Lillith]].

Following their trail north to Fervolosh, they met with Jarl Hefid of Pamfyl and the white dragon Brensekrah. After a summer spent all around the North, hunting linnorms for the next clue, a trail laid by Jeanne CLaire Lillith led them to awaken Zul, the great tarn linnorm of Åperbung, who took flight and laid waste to the surrounding settlements.
With the help of the northmen and their jarls, the Silver Flame Sentinels finally took down Zul in Pamfyl after prying off his hide a powerful healing artifact.
Infernal forces assigned to Griannimhziel joined the fray to retrieve the artifact, but were driven away by an equal celestial response.

## Olgizmar, the North, and mythic power

Bestowed with divine blessings, the companions accepted a curious invitation from [[Jeanne Claire Lillith]] to her office demiplane in [[Dorvolosh]]. She pointed them towards [[Eltirion]] and the fiendish forces besieging it.
Having established a sturdy informant network, Szoraufein and two of his agents managed to infiltrate Castle Bakkerskrone and abduct Çan Tal (the previous wielder of the Dawnblade who had long since turned into a vampire) and move him to the Elysium Guild in Ekteron where the Silver Flame Sentinels interrogated him.
Following additional leads around Dorvolosh, the band unwittingly crossed a gate into the Asphodel Fields, a dead-magic plane where they were trapped for 40 days. During that time, Çan Tal's magical constraints wore off and he escaped, and the 2nd Tense Synod took place; during the resultant strife between the magister factions, a contingent of Fråddarth forces led by Jeanne Claire Lillith happened the gate to the Asphodel Fields and set the companions free.

After traversing the Harrowlands with the help of [[Qosvahriin]], the Sentinels smashed through the ring of infernal forces and met with the lines of the Chosen on the other side. Ascending to the top, they engaged in a heated meeting with [[Angaeradh]], who nonetheless convened the task force of celestial paragons working on the Dawnblade's case.
The council included [[Yerastu]] himself, who approved the Silver Flame Sentinels to access the Dawnblade's secret location in [[Zelgerosh]] and retrieve it.

Before undertaking the long journey south, the companions requested an audience with Jeanne Claire Lillith at her office, where they attempted to assassinate her. Being a master trickster, she escaped despite their meticulous planning, though they managed to slay her secretary Lilika the Lilitu, trash her demiplane, and blink away leaving her to explain the debacle to her circle.

## The return of the Dawnblade

Trekking through the jungles of Zelgerosh , the Silver Flame Sentinels reached the Dawnblade's resting place where its Guardian dragon recognised their right to retrieve the sword as per its own demands.
With the artifact in their possession, during the first days of 4787 the companions sailed towards Erstal; there, the boneship Flying Finman rose up from the depths where it had slumbered after offloading a young [[Shizu|swordmaiden]] years earlier on the same shore.

Travelling through the aether at great speed, the boneship delivered the Silver Flame Sentinels at the ancient Selanaari harbours closest to [[Eltirion]]. As the infernal forces besieging Eltirion were now solely focused on the Dawnblade, it was too perilous to journey aboveground; thus the companions entered the dwarven highways under the mountains, and marched towards the top.
On the way, they came across the Great Western Oni Army, fleeing from the Harrowmist, and single-handedly decimated them. The battles were taking a great toll on Aaron Kunbart, however; as the wielder of the Dawnblade, the more he used it against his foes, the more the sword lashed out at him, seeping out his vitality bit by bit. 

Further up the caverns they met [[Aldastrio]], who was there at the behest of [[Angaeradh]] with the [[Rod of Stone and Steel]] to repair the structural damage to the mountain done by the desperate devils.
Shortly after they came across a luminous barrier of energy blocking the path forward, that unbeknownst to them was part of the ancient elven wards that Angaeradh had put up to slow the hellish onslaught.
Hesitant to find another way through the labyrinthine caverns on the other side, they elected to smash through the barrier. When even the most powerful of Ruthar's magic had failed to make a dent, they had Aaron strike it with the Dawnblade. The tremendous explosion that resulted took down the barrier and the entire system of ancient wards, and claimed Aaron's life. His companions hastily buried him in a cairn and kept marching on the last stretch of ground until the embattled lines of the Chosen and Angaeradh's observatory.
