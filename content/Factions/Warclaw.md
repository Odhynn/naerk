The offspring savage children of [[Karadosh]], mostly comprised of gnolls and skinwalkers. They are actively waging war in eastern [[Karrokh]] and have established themshelves in the wild areas of [[Umtahr]] and north [[Rammok Wastes]].

## History

For the first three centuries of the 4th millenia, the rule of the [[Fanged Keep|Tas-Meir]] spread to most parts of [[Karrokh]] and [[Green Hills]], after their success at the [[Battle of Twin Mountains]].
